## 1. Purpose

The Parties intend to hold a meeting on 13 November 2026 to discuss [BRIEF DESCRIPTION, e.g. a potential business collaboration / partnership / investment] (the "**Purpose**"). In doing so, each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other, and the Parties agree to protect it under this Agreement.

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one Party (the "**Discloser**") to the other (the "**Recipient**") in connection with the Purpose, in any form (oral, written, electronic, visual), whether or not marked as confidential, including: business plans and strategies, financial data, pricing, customer and partner lists, technical and software solutions, source code, algorithms, designs, know-how, business ideas, employee information, and the existence and content of discussions between the Parties.

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can demonstrate:

a) is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement;
b) was already known to the Recipient before disclosure, free of any confidentiality obligation;
c) was lawfully received from a third party not bound by a confidentiality obligation; or
d) was independently developed without use of the Confidential Information.

## 4. Obligations of the Recipient

The Recipient shall:

a) keep Confidential Information confidential, using at least the same care it uses to protect its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care;
b) use it solely for the Purpose;
c) not disclose it to any third party without the Discloser's prior written consent;
d) limit access to those employees, contractors and advisers (e.g. lawyers, accountants) who need it for the Purpose and are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations; the Recipient is responsible for their compliance;
e) not copy, record or reproduce Confidential Information except as necessary for the Purpose; and
f) promptly notify the Discloser of any unauthorised disclosure or use.

## 5. Compelled Disclosure

If the Recipient is required by law, court order or a competent authority to disclose Confidential Information, it shall (where legally permitted) give the Discloser prior notice, disclose only the portion legally required, and cooperate in protecting its confidentiality.

## 6. Ownership; No License

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. Nothing in this Agreement grants any intellectual property right or license, express or implied. Neither Party is obliged to disclose any particular information or to enter into any further agreement.

## 7. Return and Destruction

Upon the Discloser's written request, and in any case within [7] days, the Recipient shall return or destroy all materials containing Confidential Information (including copies and notes) and confirm this in writing on request. The Recipient may retain copies to the extent required by law, and this Agreement continues to apply to them.

## 8. Term

This Agreement takes effect on signature. The confidentiality obligations last for **[three (3)] years** from the date of signature and, for trade secrets and source code, continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

## 9. Liability and Remedies

The Recipient is liable for damages resulting from a breach of this Agreement. The Parties acknowledge that a breach may cause harm that is difficult to remedy, and the Discloser may therefore seek injunctive relief in addition to damages.

## 10. Personal Data

If Confidential Information contains personal data, the Parties shall process it in accordance with the data protection law applicable to each Party (for Party B, Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR); for Party A, the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection).

## 11. General Provisions

1. This Agreement is the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter. Amendments are valid only in writing signed by both Parties.
2. If any provision is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect.
3. Failure to exercise a right is not a waiver of that right.
4. This Agreement is governed by the law of the country of the Party against which a claim is brought (the defendant): the laws of the **Republic of Serbia** if the defendant is Party A, and the laws of **Romania** if the defendant is Party B. Any dispute shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent court in **Vršac, Serbia**, if the defendant is Party A, and of the competent court in **Timișoara, Romania**, if the defendant is Party B, after an attempt at amicable resolution.
5. This Agreement is made in two (2) identical copies, one for each Party (or may be signed electronically).
